Our story

Built at the crossroads of Bushbuckridge.

Situated at the key intersection of the R40 and R533, within the bustling Bushbuckridge CBD, the mall spans 7.46 hectares and offers a carefully curated mix of national retailers and local businesses — providing convenience and variety to the community.

Bushbuckridge Mall is managed by Jeqe Management, whose hands-on team keeps the centre safe, clean and thriving, seven days a week.

Our vision

The mall goes beyond being a shopping destination — it represents our dedication to the local community and our aspirations for a brighter future. An inclusive, modern and lively space for every visitor.

Community engagement

We actively engage with local organisations and initiatives to make a positive impact. As part of the Bushbuckridge community, we believe in giving back and supporting the community that has embraced us.

Sustainability & care

With a commitment to minimising our environmental impact and promoting sustainable development in the region, we proactively explore eco-friendly initiatives and support local causes aligned with our values.
